Access code

You received the code from your coaches. You enter it once when opening the app. After that, the app remembers it on this device.

Add to home screen

  1. Open this page in Safari. It does not work inside other apps such as WhatsApp or Instagram.
  2. Tap Open app and enter your code.
  3. In the opened app, tap the Share icon at the bottom (square with an arrow pointing up).
  4. Choose Add to Home Screen and tap Add.
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Open app and enter your code.
  3. In the opened app, tap the menu with the three dots at the top right.
  4. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Afterwards the app sits on your home screen with its own icon. Your entries stay on your device. If you clear browser data or change devices, the app starts from scratch. Use the backup in the app to move your data.

Good to know

The app accompanies your coaching and does not replace medical or psychotherapeutic treatment. If you have questions, write to your coaches.
